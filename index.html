Brief Bio

I am a graduate student in the Lab of Computational Biology at POSTECH, advised by Prof. Jong Kyoung Kim. I received my B.S. in Life Sciences from POSTECH in June 2023, graduating summa cum laude.

My current work focuses on:

  • Multi-omics analysis of lung cancer
  • Developing methods for single-cell atlas analysis
